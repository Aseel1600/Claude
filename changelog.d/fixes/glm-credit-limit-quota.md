- **fix(usage):** z.ai/GLM coding-plan subscription keys now render their quota cards again. Z.ai's `/api/monitor/usage/quota/limit` switched these keys from `TOKENS_LIMIT` to `CREDIT_LIMIT` rows (same `unit`/`number` semantics: unit=3/number=5 → 5-hour window, unit=6/number=1 → weekly), and the parser only matched `TOKENS_LIMIT`/`TIME_LIMIT`, so both rows were dropped and the subscription card rendered empty. `CREDIT_LIMIT` is now accepted alongside `TOKENS_LIMIT`
